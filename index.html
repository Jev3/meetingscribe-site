Privacy, concretely

Three things happen to your meeting. One of them touches the internet.

The audio

Stays on your disk

Written to your own drive and transcribed by your own processor. It is never uploaded — there is no server of ours to upload it to. Delete the meeting and the file goes with it.

The transcript

Stays on your disk

Kept in a local database on your computer, searchable with no connection at all. Export it, move it, delete it — it is a file you own.

The summary

Leaves only when you click

The transcript text goes to the Anthropic API on your own key, and a confirmation shows exactly what is about to be sent. The recording is never part of it.

Your API key is stored in the Windows Credential Manager, not in a config file, and can be removed from Settings at any time.

Questions

Before you download.

Do I need a microphone?

No. MeetingScribe records what your computer plays, so every remote participant is captured even through headphones. Your own microphone is not recorded — if you need your side of the call too, turn on your app's self-monitoring or play the call through your speakers.

Does anything get uploaded?

The audio never does. The transcript text is sent to Anthropic only when you ask for a summary, using your own API key, and only after a confirmation screen showing what is included.

Do I need an internet connection?

Twice: to download a speech model the first time, and for AI summaries. Recording, transcription, search and export work fully offline.

Why my own Anthropic key?

So the summaries stay between you and Anthropic: requests are billed to your account, and nothing passes through a server of ours. Create a key at console.anthropic.com and paste it into Settings.

Windows warns about an unknown publisher.

The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning on first run. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Some antivirus tools flag the transcription engine for the same reason — restore the file and add the folder to exclusions.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. MeetingScribe runs on Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

How do I get my licence key?

It arrives by email right after checkout. Paste it into Settings → License and Pro activates immediately, on this machine, offline.
